Field Service Operations Review

Northern Region · Third Quarter 2026 · Prepared by Operations Planning

1. Summary

Service volume in the Northern Region rose through the quarter while the average time to close a ticket fell for the third consecutive reporting period. The two depots brought online in May absorbed most of the additional volume, and overtime hours returned to their pre-expansion level by the end of September.

2. Findings

2.1 What improved

  • First-visit resolution rose to 82%, up from 74% in the second quarter.
  • Parts availability at the depot level reached 91% against a target of 88%.
  • Overtime fell to 4.1% of scheduled hours, the lowest figure since 2024.

2.2 What did not

  • Scheduling accuracy for afternoon appointments remained below target at 79%.
  • Two depots reported van stock discrepancies that took more than a week to reconcile.

4. Recommended actions

  1. Move afternoon appointment windows from three hours to two, starting in November.
  2. Run a stock reconciliation at Calder Vale and Eastgate before the end of October.
  3. Hold depot headcount flat through the fourth quarter and review in January.

5. Next review

The next operations review covers October to December and is scheduled for 16 January 2027. Depot managers should submit their figures by 9 January.
